Modern Adaptive Cruise Control (ACC) holds a driver-selected cruising speed when the highway ahead is clear, and automatically modulates throttle and braking to maintain a speed-proportional safe time-gap whenever slower traffic enters the lane. Our scaled 4WD prototype brings this automotive ADAS architecture to life on an ESP32 microcontroller.

01 • Real-World Automotive Applications

How Adaptive Cruise Control Transforms Everyday Driving

From multi-lane expressways to congested city corridors, ACC automates longitudinal speed control to prevent rear-end collisions, smooth traffic waves, and cut driver fatigue.

CRUISE MODE

Highway Cruising & Fatigue Reduction

Maintains a steady driver-selected expressway speed when the lane ahead is clear, then gently backs off the throttle when approaching slower vehicles—eliminating constant pedal adjustments during long journeys.

Closed-Loop Velocity Hold
FOLLOW & STOP

Stop-and-Go Urban Traffic Assist

Automatically tracks decelerating traffic down to a smooth, complete halt at a guaranteed standstill buffer (d₀), and smoothly resumes acceleration as soon as the lead vehicle moves ahead.

Standstill Safety Buffer
E-STOP GUARD

Emergency Collision Mitigation (Cut-In Response)

Detects sudden lane cut-ins or abrupt braking by a lead vehicle within milliseconds—far faster than human reaction time—immediately cutting throttle and commanding full emergency deceleration.

20–40 ms Hazard Response
STRING STABILITY

Traffic Flow & Energy Efficiency

Constant time-gap spacing paired with acceleration slew-rate limiting dampens phantom "accordion" traffic waves across highway convoys, reducing unnecessary hard braking and improving fuel and EV battery efficiency.

Damped Smooth Acceleration

Signal Conditioning

Exponential Moving Average (EMA) Filter

Raw ToF laser and ultrasonic range measurements suffer from occasional surface scatter and echo jitter. We filter distance every 20 ms tick before it enters the outer gap loop:

dfilt[k] = 0.7 · dfilt[k−1] + 0.3 · draw[k]
  • Zero Memory Overhead: Single-state recursive IIR filter ideal for embedded 50 Hz ESP32 loops.
  • Fast Obstacle Response: Weight α = 0.3 suppresses jitter spikes while reaching 90% of a real step change in under 140 ms.
  • Smooth PWM Braking: Prevents high-frequency sensor noise from chattering motor drivers.

04 • Supervisory Control & Fail-Safes

Deterministic 4-State Machine & 3-Tier Safety Defense

Every 20 ms control cycle evaluates hardware safety interrupts, sensor validity, and headway thresholds to enforce unambiguous mode transitions.

STATE 01

IDLE

Entry: Boot-up or cruise setpoint < 5 cm/s

  • ▸Motors commanded to 0 PWM.
  • ▸PID integral accumulator reset to zero.
  • ▸OLED shows standby telemetry.
STATE 02

CRUISE

Entry: Clear road (d > d_des + margin)

  • ▸Green status LED illuminated.
  • ▸Inner PID tracks driver cruise setpoint.
  • ▸Outer gap loop cleanly bypassed.
STATE 03

FOLLOW

Entry: Lead car detected (d ≤ d_des + margin)

  • ▸Amber status LED illuminated.
  • ▸Outer gap loop sets target velocity.
  • ▸Slew limiter ensures smooth speed match.
STATE 04

E-STOP

Entry: d < 20 cm, Sensor Fault, or Kill Switch

  • ▸Red LED + OLED emergency alert.
  • ▸Immediate 0 PWM cut & PID reset.
  • ▸Overrides all modes unconditionally.

Three-Tier Hardware & Software Safety Defense

Layered protection ensures a single sensor glitch or firmware lockup can never cause a collision

TIER 01 • REDUNDANCY

Dual Distance Sensing

Primary VL53L0X laser ToF is cross-checked against an HC-SR04 ultrasonic backup. A strict 15 ms echo timeout keeps the 50 Hz loop deterministic even on open roads.

TIER 02 • WATCHDOG

Sensor-Fault Latch (>0.5 s)

Tracks consecutive invalid or disconnected readings. Exceeding 25 fault cycles (0.5 s at 50 Hz) automatically latches E-STOP so the vehicle never drives blind.

TIER 03 • HARDWARE CUTOFF

Physical Kill Switch & Fuse

A top-mounted rear emergency kill switch paired with an inline battery fuse provides instant physical power isolation completely independent of software.

05 • Embedded Platform & Mechanical CAD

Prototype Hardware Stack, 3D Packaging & Virtual Demo

Engineered as a modular, two-deck 4WD test platform designed in Autodesk Fusion 360 and validated end-to-end in Tinkercad Circuits.

Compute Core

ESP32 Microcontroller

Executes the deterministic 50 Hz (20 ms) control loop, handles high-speed wheel encoder GPIO interrupts, and streams live serial/Wi-Fi telemetry for PID tuning.

Forward Perception

VL53L0X ToF + HC-SR04 Backup

Narrow-beam Time-of-Flight laser delivers fast, accurate primary gap readings over I2C, backed up by an ultrasonic sensor for dark or angled surfaces.

Drivetrain & Odometry

4WD Encoder Motors & H-Bridge

Four geared DC motors with quadrature encoders measure real-time wheel velocity, driven by an H-bridge PWM motor driver with exposed heatsink cooling.

Inertial Sensing

MPU6050 6-Axis IMU

Mounted at the chassis geometric center to track longitudinal braking deceleration, detect wheel slip, and minimize yaw lever-arm measurement error.

Power & Isolation

Battery, Buck, Fuse & Kill Switch

Centered battery pack feeds motors and an isolated DC-DC buck converter for clean 5V/3.3V logic rails, protected by an inline fuse and rear manual kill switch.

Operator HMI

0.96" I2C OLED & Setpoint Knob

Angled OLED bezel acts as an onboard instrument cluster displaying Mode, Distance, Speed, and PWM, paired with a rotary potentiometer to adjust cruise speed live.

ZONE 1 • FRONT NOSE ("THE EYES")

Unobstructed Dual-Sensor Bracket

Dedicated L-bracket mounts both the VL53L0X ToF optical window and dual HC-SR04 ultrasonic transducers flush ahead of the front wheels—preventing tire interference and false floor echoes.

ZONE 2 • LOWER DECK ("MUSCLES & LOW CG")

Drivetrain Mass & Braking Pitch Control

Recessed wheel wells clear the 65 mm tires while strapping the battery pack right down the center keeps the center of gravity low so the chassis stays flat during hard E-Stop braking.

ZONE 3 • UPPER DECK ("BRAIN, DASHBOARD & KILL SWITCH")

EMI Isolation, Airflow & Instant Safety Access

Lifted 45 mm on six brass M3 standoffs with hex weight-reduction cutouts. Houses the ESP32, exposed heatsink motor driver, OLED telemetry bezel, and rear mushroom kill switch.
